## 1. Accessibility Page Content HTML

```html
<h2>Our commitment</h2>
<p>We design and build shopwanderwell.co to meet the <strong>Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA</strong>. That's the same standard recommended by the U.S. Department of Justice for compliance with Title III of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).</p>

<h2>What we've done</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Keyboard navigation.</strong> Every link, button, form field, and product card can be reached and activated with the keyboard alone. A visible focus ring shows where you are.</li>
  <li><strong>Screen reader support.</strong> Headings, landmarks, image alt text, and form labels are written for assistive technology. Interactive controls announce their state.</li>
  <li><strong>Color contrast.</strong> Text meets a 4.5:1 contrast ratio (3:1 for large headings) against its background.</li>
  <li><strong>Reduced motion.</strong> If you've turned on "reduce motion" in your operating system, our animations, tickers, and card flips quiet down automatically.</li>
```
